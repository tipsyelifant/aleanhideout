Gemba means “the real place” — where the work actually happens.

A Gemba Walk is when you go to observe the real process with your own eyes, not rely on reports or guesses.

🛠️ Try this in your own work!

What is Value Stream Mapping?

Value Stream Mapping (VSM) helps you see the full process — from start to finish — and spot where things get stuck.

It shows which steps add value for the customer… and which ones don’t.

By mapping it out, teams can uncover:

  • Delays
  • Rework
  • Bottlenecks

In Lean, the goal is to focus on value-adding steps — and cut out the waste.

🛠️ Try this in your own work!

  • 🗺️ Map out the full process — from start to finish
  • 🏃‍♀️ Circle the steps that clearly add value for the customer
  • ❌ Put a cross on the ones that feel wasteful, duplicated, or unclear
  • 🧠 Ask: “What’s essential — and what’s just noise?”
