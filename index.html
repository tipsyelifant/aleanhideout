🧠 Did you know?

What is Value Stream Mapping?

Value Stream Mapping (VSM) helps you see the full process — from start to finish — and spot where things get stuck.

It shows which steps add value for the customer… and which ones don’t.

By mapping it out, teams can uncover:

  • Delays
  • Rework
  • Bottlenecks

In Lean, the goal is to focus on value-adding steps — and cut out the waste.

🛠️ Try this in your own work!

  • 🗺️ Map out the full process — from start to finish
  • 🏃‍♀️ Circle the steps that clearly add value for the customer
  • ❌ Put a cross on the ones that feel wasteful, duplicated, or unclear
  • 🧠 Ask: “What’s essential — and what’s just noise?”

🧠 Did You Know?

Lead Time is your total journey time (like a food delivery from order to your doorstep), Process Time is the actual work time (cooking the food), and Wait Time is all the delays in between (waiting for the cook to start, food waiting for delivery pickup)!

In most processes, actual work (Process Time) takes up only 10% of the total time, while 90% is just waiting! Imagine a burger order that takes 30 minutes to reach you – the actual cooking might only be 3 minutes. The rest? That’s waiting time!

🛠 Try this in your own work!

It is important for Heidi to understand the timing in her kitchen operations. While orders take 45 minutes to reach customers (Lead Time), the actual cooking (Process Time) might only be 15 minutes – making her wonder where the other 30 minutes go (Wait Time).

Want to discover where time really goes in your work? Try tracking three simple times: how long customers wait in total (Lead Time), how long the actual work takes (Process Time), and how long things sit waiting (Wait Time). Pick one process and time these for just five orders. You'll be surprised to find how much time is spent waiting versus doing actual work!

Did You Know?

TIMWOODI represents the 8 common types of waste in any process: Transport, Inventory, Motion, Waiting, Over-production, Over-processing, Defects, and Intellect. These wastes often hide in plain sight, eating up time and resources in your workplace!

For example, in Heidi's kitchen, moving ingredients back and forth (Transport), inefficient task distribution (Intellect), unnecessary movements (Motion), or faulty stoves (Defects) all add to wasted time and effort.

Try this at work!

Spot the waste in your workplace:

  • Pick one process you do daily
  • Watch for any TIMWOODI wastes
  • You'll be surprised how many hidden wastes you can find once you know what to look for!
