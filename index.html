Congratulations! The answer is GEMBA.

GEMBA is a Japanese term that means "the real place." In Lean, it refers to the practice of going to the actual place where work is done to see the process, understand the work, and identify opportunities for improvement.

How can you apply GEMBA in your work?

  • Visit the frontlines to understand the challenges your colleagues face.
  • Shadow a colleague for a day to see their work from their perspective.
  • Talk to customers to get their feedback on your services.
